## 摘要
随着 AI 编程智能体变得更加自主，开发者越来越多地指导、协调并检查其工作。然而，人们对日常专业开发中的这些实践仍知之甚少。我们跟随观察了 11 名专业开发者约 15 小时的工作，对其活动和任务转换进行编码，分析了 255 条提交的提示，并进行了 4.6 小时的后续访谈。开发者在观察时间内平均有 33.4% 用于构思提示或检查 AI 响应，任务切换率从每小时 0 次到 101.6 次不等。除产出制品外，提示还支持理解、需求澄清、智能体配置和协调。我们识别出三种反复出现的模式：监督者（Supervisor），涉及直接检查制品；委派者（Delegator），涉及基于对话的协调以及委派的实现与检查；学习者（Learner），主要将智能体用于构建理解。开发者从智能体处获取技术信息，从同事处获取组织背景、权威和对齐。我们的发现提示未来应研究开发者如何在并发的智能体任务之间管理注意力，以及支持验证、协调和学习的工具。

## Abstract
As AI coding agents become more autonomous, developers increasingly direct, coordinate, and check their work. Yet little is known about these practices in everyday professional development. We shadowed 11 professional developers across approximately 15 hours of work, coded their activities and task transitions, analyzed 255 submitted prompts, and conducted 4.6 hours of follow-up interviews.   Developers spent an average of 33.4% of observed time formulating prompts or checking AI responses, with task-switching rates ranging from 0 to 101.6 switches per hour. Beyond producing artifacts, prompts supported understanding, requirements clarification, agent configuration, and coordination. We identified three recurring patterns: \textit{Supervisor}, involving direct artifact inspection; \textit{Delegator}, involving dialogue-based coordination and delegated implementation and checking; and \textit{Learner}, involving agents primarily to build understanding. Developers sought technical information from agents and organizational context, authority, and alignment from colleagues. Our findings suggest future studies of how developers manage attention across concurrent agent tasks and tools that support verification, coordination, and learning.
