## 摘要
生成式人工智能（GenAI）正日益融入软件工程教育，支持需求开发、设计探索、文档编写和原型设计等活动。然而，教育者通常只能看到最终制品，对于学生在学习过程中如何评估、验证和完善AI生成的输出，了解有限。这为评估评价性判断和负责任的AI辅助实践带来了挑战。本文介绍了AI Journal，一种结构化反思框架，旨在使一年级软件工程教育中的学生与GenAI互动变得可见。该框架将执行跟踪与认知审计相结合：执行跟踪记录提示、输出、意图和交互情境；认知审计则捕捉验证策略、干预决策、信心判断、关键学习时刻以及对AI支持工作的反思。AI Journal被部署在一门第一学期软件工程课程中，使人们能够看到仅通过基于制品的评估无法观察到的学生学习方面。初步观察表明，学生在验证实践、干预策略以及对AI支持工作的看法方面存在差异。关键学习时刻常常出现在学生评估情境适宜性、可行性以及与需求的一致性时，而不是在识别明显错误时。AI Journal展示了一种实用、轻量且与模型无关的方法，使AI辅助学习过程变得可见。通过突出验证、干预和反思，它将关注点从以产品为中心的评估转向评价性判断和负责任的AI辅助实践。

## Abstract
Generative AI (GenAI) is increasingly embedded in software engineering education, supporting activities such as requirements development, design exploration, documentation, and prototyping. However, educators often have visibility only into final artefacts, with limited insight into how students evaluate, verify, and refine AI-generated outputs during the learning process. This creates challenges for assessing evaluative judgement and responsible AI-assisted practice. This paper introduces the AI Journal, a structured reflection framework designed to make student-GenAI interaction visible in first-year software engineering education. The framework combines execution tracking, which records prompts, outputs, intent, and interaction context, with cognitive auditing, which captures verification strategies, intervention decisions, confidence judgements, critical learning moments, and reflections on AI-supported work. Deployed in a first-semester software engineering course, the AI Journal enabled visibility into aspects of student learning not observable through artefact-based assessments alone. Preliminary observations suggested variation in verification practices, intervention strategies, and perceptions of AI-supported work. Critical learning moments frequently occurred when students evaluated contextual suitability, feasibility, and requirements alignment rather than identifying obvious errors. The AI Journal demonstrates a practical, lightweight, and model-agnostic approach for making AI-assisted learning processes visible. By foregrounding verification, intervention, and reflection, it shifts attention from product-focused assessment toward evaluative judgement and responsible AI-assisted practice.
