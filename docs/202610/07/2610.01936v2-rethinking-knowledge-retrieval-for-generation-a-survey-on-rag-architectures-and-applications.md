## 摘要
大语言模型（LLMs）在自然语言任务中展现出卓越的流畅性与通用性，但其静态知识以及易产生幻觉的问题仍构成根本性限制，尤其是在需要最新信息或基于属性依据的信息的领域。检索增强生成（RAG）通过将外部检索机制与生成模型相结合来应对这些挑战，使生成能够动态、上下文感知地基于可验证数据源。本综述全面考察了RAG作为一种模块化且不断演进的范式，如何提升基于LLM系统的事实可靠性、适应性与任务对齐能力。我们围绕检索、生成与增强这三个基础组件对RAG框架进行形式化，并综述了涵盖稠密与稀疏检索器、融合策略、嵌入优化以及基于强化学习的检索策略的前沿方法。以效率、安全性、以用户为中心的交互性和复杂推理这四个新兴维度为锚点，我们对近期创新进行分类，并强调其对可扩展性、鲁棒性和个性化的启示。本文还回顾了评估协议、领域特定应用以及Naïve RAG、Advanced RAG和Modular RAG等架构变体的进展。最后，我们指出持续存在的挑战，并勾勒未来方向，旨在推进检索与大语言模型的融合，以实现更具依据、可解释和可控的生成。

## Abstract
Large Language Models (LLMs) have demonstrated remarkable fluency and versatility across natural language tasks but remain fundamentally limited by their static knowledge and susceptibility to hallucinations, especially in domains requiring up to date or attribute grounded information. Retrieval Augmented Generation (RAG) addresses these challenges by integrating external retrieval mechanisms with generative models, enabling dynamic, context aware generation grounded in verifiable data sources. This survey presents a comprehensive examination of RAG as a modular and evolving paradigm that enhances factual reliability, adaptability, and task alignment in LLM based systems. We formalize the RAG framework through its three foundational components retrieval, generation, and augmentation and survey state of the art methods spanning dense and sparse retrievers, fusion strategies, embedding optimizations, and reinforcement learning based retrieval policies. Anchored around four emerging axes efficiency, security, user centric interactivity, and complex reasoning we categorize recent innovations and highlight their implications for scalability, robustness, and personalization. The paper also reviews advances in evaluation protocols, domain specific applications, and architectural variants such as Naïve RAG, Advanced RAG, and Modular RAG. Finally, we identify persistent challenges and outline future directions aimed at advancing the integration of retrieval with LLMs for more grounded, interpretable, and controllable generation.
