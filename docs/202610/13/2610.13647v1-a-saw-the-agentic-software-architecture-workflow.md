## 摘要
软件工程中的人工智能辅助主要集中在实现和验证阶段，而设计阶段——尤其是架构文档的维护——则相对受到忽视。架构即代码（Architecture as Code）使架构制品可版本化、可差异比较且机器可读，但它并未减少保持这些制品更新的工作量，因此设计文档最终仍然成为碎片化、依赖作者、且仅被部分信任的记录，用以说明系统为何呈现当前形态。在本文中，我们提出智能体软件架构工作流（Agentic Software Architecture Workflow, A-SAW），这是一种流程级制品，它通过一个持久化的智能体层扩展了架构即代码：一个集成于 IDE 的智能体从受版本控制的 C4 模型、类型化元素目录和插桩模板中起草架构文档。每一份草稿在被接受之前都必须经过强制性的人工审查关口。我们在一家大型工业组织中沿两条路径对其进行评估：对 45 份架构文档进行评分量规评估，其中包括 13 对匹配的源文档-再生成文档；以及 13 次任务锚定的从业者访谈。我们的结果表明，该工作流消除了导致质量不可预测的作者间差异。内在质量也有所提升，在匹配对上中位数提高了 +19 分。该队列在捆绑模板、目录、智能体以及更丰富的再生成上下文时，平均为 58% 和 89%。因此，它们为智能体的贡献给出了上界，而不是将其单独分离出来。在智能体生成的文档中，完整性（100%）、一致性（93%）和机器可读性（99%）达到或接近评分量规的最高值，而可追溯性达到 79%。13 名参与者中有 11 人拒绝未经人工审查的智能体输出，还有一人警告说，经过润色的输出可能会导致审查者跳过审查。我们的工作支持保持人工审查为强制性要求，并将其集中在智能体必须超越仓库进行推断的地方。

## Abstract
AI assistance in software engineering focuses on implementation and verification, leaving the design phase, and in particular the maintenance of architecture documentation, comparatively underserved. Architecture as Code makes architectural artifacts versionable, diffable, and machine-readable, but it does not reduce the effort of keeping them updated, so design documentation still ends up as a fragmented, author-dependent, and only partially trusted record of why a system looks the way it does.   In this paper we introduce the Agentic Software Architecture Workflow (A-SAW), a process-level artifact that extends Architecture as Code with a persistent agent layer: an IDE-integrated agent drafts architecture documents from a version-controlled C4 model, a typed element catalog, and instrumented templates. Every draft passes a mandatory human review gate before it is accepted. We evaluate it in a large industrial organization along two tracks: a rubric assessment of 45 architecture documents including 13 matched source-regeneration pairs, and 13 task-anchored practitioner interviews. Our results show that the workflow collapses the between-author variance that made quality unpredictable. Intrinsic quality also rises, by a median of +19 points across the matched pair. The cohort has a mean of 58% and 89% bundle templates, catalog, agent, and a richer regeneration context. So they bound the agent's contribution from above rather than isolating it. In the agent-generated documents, completeness (100%), consistency (93%), and machine-readability (99%) reach or approach the rubric maximum, while traceability reaches 79%. Eleven of the 13 participants rejected agent output that no human had reviewed, and one warned that polished output may lead reviewers to skip the review. Our work supports keeping human review mandatory and concentrating it where the agent must infer beyond the repository.
