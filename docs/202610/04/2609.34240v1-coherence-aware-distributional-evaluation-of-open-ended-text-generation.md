## 摘要
现有的开放式文本生成指标在通用表示空间中衡量似然、词汇多样性或分布相似性，但它们可能遗漏一些基本的质量维度。一个突出的盲点是全局连贯性：生成的段落可能局部流畅，但全局上却自相矛盾、因果不一致或主题脱节。此类失败仍可能保留现有指标所依赖的词元级和词汇统计特征。我们将表示识别为检测这些失败的核心瓶颈，并提出 CHORD（Coherence-aware Hidden-state Open-generation Reference Distance，连贯性感知隐状态开放生成参考距离），一种对连贯性敏感的分布度量。CHORD 使用能引发连贯性的提示，将生成语料和人类撰写语料编码到冻结 LLM 的隐状态空间中，并使用带 RBF 核的 MMD 比较所得分布。为验证该度量会对连贯性退化而非一般文本变化做出响应，我们构建了一个反事实评估套件，将分级连贯性退化扰动与保持语义的对照配对。CHORD 能有选择地检测关系、语篇、结构和混合型失败，而困惑度、熵、MAUVE、FBD 以及基于 MMD 的基线要么会遗漏这些失败，要么无法将其与良性改写区分开。因子消融实验表明，表示是连贯性敏感性的主要来源，而一旦相关区分变得可见，RBF-MMD 会提高样本效率。更大的骨干模型能捕捉更细粒度的区分，但只有当骨干模型能够遵循提示时，连贯性提示才能提高选择性。在无条件生成和前缀续写任务上，CHORD 给出的模型排名与人类关于输出是否合理、是否像人类撰写的判断高度一致。总之，这些结果表明表示设计对于可靠的分布评估至关重要。

## Abstract
Existing metrics for open-ended text generation measure likelihood, lexical diversity, or distributional similarity in generic representation space, yet they can miss fundamental dimensions of quality. A prominent blind spot is global coherence: a generated passage may be locally fluent while remaining globally contradictory, causally inconsistent, or topically disconnected. Such failures can still preserve the token-level and lexical statistics that existing metrics rely on. We identify representation as a central bottleneck in detecting these failures and introduce CHORD (Coherence-aware Hidden-state Open-generation Reference Distance), a coherence-sensitive distributional metric. CHORD encodes generated and human-written corpora in the hidden-state space of a frozen LLM using a coherence-eliciting prompt, and compares the resulting distributions using MMD with an RBF kernel. To validate that the metric responds to coherence degradation but not generic textual change, we construct a counterfactual evaluation suite that pairs graded coherence-degrading perturbations with meaning-preserving controls. CHORD selectively detects relation, discourse, structural, and mixture failures that perplexity, entropy, MAUVE, FBD, and MMD-based baselines either miss or cannot separate from benign rewriting. Factorial ablations show that representation is the primary source of coherence sensitivity,while RBF-MMD improves sample efficiency once the relevant distinctions become visible. Larger backbones capture finer-grained distinctions, but coherence prompting improves selectivity only when the backbone can follow the prompt.On unconditional generation and prefix continuation, CHORD yields model rankings that strongly align with human judgments of whether outputs make sense and appear human-written. Together, these results establish representation design as central to reliable distributional evaluation.
